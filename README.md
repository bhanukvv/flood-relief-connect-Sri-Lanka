# Flood Relief Connect 🇱🇰

**Flood Relief Connect** is a real-time platform designed to connect flood victims, volunteers, donors, and collection centers across Sri Lanka. Our goal is to streamline disaster relief and make it accessible, transparent, and efficient.

---

## 🌟 Features

- **Live Map Visualization**: See who needs help, who wants to help, and where collection centers are located.
- **Easy Requests**: Submit requests for assistance, register as a volunteer, or add a collection center.
- **Real-Time Updates**: All pins update live, allowing quick and direct connections.
- **Direct Communication**: Contact people via phone or WhatsApp, and get directions using Google Maps.

---

## 📍 Map Pin Explanation

- **🔴 Need Help**: People urgently needing assistance.
- **🟢 Want to Help**: Volunteers or donors offering support.
- **🔵 Collection Centers**: Locations accepting donations.

---

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. **Submit Your Request**: Fill out the form at the Help Center.
2. **Appear on the Map**: Your location shows up immediately as a colored pin.
3. **Connect & Help**: Others can contact you directly or navigate to your location.

---

## 💡 Our Goal

- Speed up response times during flood emergencies
- Connect victims directly with nearby helpers
- Reduce coordination overhead for relief efforts
- Make assistance accessible to everyone
- Build a stronger, more resilient community
